Where to Buy Watches Online That Ship Internationally (2026)

Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

A good watch is one of the few accessories that holds its value and never goes out of style. The catch, when buying online, is finding a seller that ships to your country and backs up what it's selling. This guide covers where to buy watches that ship internationally, and how to do it safely.

Why watches are tricky to buy across borders

High-value and easily counterfeited, watches reward caution. Many brand boutiques restrict regions; customs can add to the price; and for pre-owned, authenticity is everything. The simplest route is to shop stores that already ship worldwide and price in your currency — which is what we feature across our watches edit and brands.

New vs pre-owned

  • New from a worldwide-shipping retailer gets you full warranty and the retailer's returns policy.
  • Pre-owned can offer real value on higher-end pieces — but only with proper authentication, box and papers where possible, and clear condition photos.
  • Fashion watches are a low-risk way to get a quality look without an investment outlay.

How to buy a watch online safely

  • Confirm authenticity — buy new from authorised sellers, or pre-owned only with authentication and documentation.
  • Check shipping, insurance & customs — high-value parcels should be insured; duties may apply.
  • Read the returns policy before committing.
  • Shop in your currency — prices here convert, so the total is clear upfront.
