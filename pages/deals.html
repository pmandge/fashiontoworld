Frequently Asked Questions

Do these stores ship worldwide — including to India?

Yes. Every retailer we feature ships internationally — to 180+ countries, India included. You browse the deals here, then check out on the store's own site, which handles delivery to your address.

Can I see prices in my own currency or in ₹?

Prices are shown in your local currency, including Indian rupees (₹). Figures are pulled from the partner stores and can change, so the store's own site always shows the final price at checkout.

Is Fashion to World a shop, or do I buy somewhere else?

We're a discovery platform, not a store. We gather deals and verified deals from trusted retailers in one place; you complete your purchase securely on the retailer's own website. We may earn a commission when you buy — at no extra cost to you.

How often is the deals updated?

Daily. New arrivals and fresh markdowns are refreshed every day across the stores we track.
