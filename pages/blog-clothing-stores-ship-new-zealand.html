Shopping fashion online from New Zealand has one recurring frustration: you find the perfect piece, reach checkout, and the store won't ship to you — or the freight costs more than the item. This guide is about avoiding that entirely by shopping stores that genuinely deliver to NZ.

The "ships to New Zealand" problem

A lot of style content quietly assumes you're in the US or UK. For NZ shoppers, the useful filter isn't "what's trending" — it's "what will actually arrive." Every store we feature ships worldwide, New Zealand included, and prices convert to NZD so there are no checkout surprises. Start from the women's and men's edits to see the range.

What to know about GST & customs

  • GST on low-value imports is generally collected at checkout by registered overseas retailers, so you often pay it upfront rather than on delivery.
  • Higher-value orders can attract duties — factor this into the total on big buys.
  • Delivery windows to NZ are longer than domestic; order ahead for events.
